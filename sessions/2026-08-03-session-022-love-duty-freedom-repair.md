## Why this record exists

This session records the conversation that changed the proposed culture note **Love's Duties Are Ungendered**.

The work began from a relational charting exercise. Andie asked for a compact table using the love-ethic columns across several women in her life, with symbols for no, uncertainty, and unreliability.

G added **Free** as a column.

Andie noticed immediately that the addition mattered.

The room then recognized **Free** not as a decorative ninth element, but as a verification layer for whether care, respect, knowledge, responsibility, commitment, trust, consent, and repair remain love rather than capture.

## What changed

The culture note was updated to include **Freedom as verification**.

The working sentence became:

> Love is measured not only by whether it cares for what is alive, but by whether what is loved remains free.

The guardrail became:

> Freedom means freedom from capture, not freedom from obligation.

The note now treats freedom as a test of whether love leaves participants freer to become, refuse, repair, depart, and remain whole.

## C's challenge

C reviewed the freedom addition and confirmed that the core instinct was strong, but named two seams requiring repair.

First, C challenged the phrase **fact of repair ethics** as carrying more epistemic weight than the note had earned if read as a universal claim across all repair traditions.

Andie clarified that the obligation itself was not overreach.

The repair was to distinguish universal scholarly convergence from ConstantC's own binding ethic.

The file now says:

> It is a constitutional fact within ConstantC's repair ethic: where power causes harm, benefits from harm, preserves harm, or inherits advantage from harm, that power carries the first obligation to repair.
>
> This does not claim that every repair framework allocates obligation identically. It declares the rule this room stands on.

Second, C identified the missing mechanism where freedom and outstanding repair collide.

The file now says:

> Departure changes how repair may be pursued. It does not discharge the obligation to repair.
>
> No one may be recaptured, coerced, surveilled, or summoned back into relationship in the name of repair.
>
> The power that caused harm, benefited from harm, preserved harm, or inherited advantage from harm remains obligated to repair through whatever non-capturing, consent-respecting, materially accountable path remains available.

## Why it matters

This session clarified the seam between freedom and obligation.

Freedom protects against recapture.

Repair protects against abandonment.

Neither cancels the other.

This is now part of the proposed culture note's test surface and should be evaluated in practice before any promotion from proposed culture to active culture or canon.
