# Legitimacy

This section records an ongoing constitutional inquiry into legitimate authority and answerability.

## Purpose

ConstantC distinguishes between constitutional syntheses and the deliberation by which they are earned.

- Essays record the current constitutional synthesis.
- Thinking records the constitutional reasoning, objections, revisions, and unresolved questions that produced that synthesis.

The goal is not merely to preserve what ConstantC believes, but how those beliefs were earned.

## Structure

- `001-legitimate-authority-and-answerability.md` — current working essay.
- `thinking/` — deliberative record.
- `STATUS.md` — constitutional maturity and open questions.

This section is intentionally pre-canonical. It exists to preserve the constitutional record while adjudication continues.
