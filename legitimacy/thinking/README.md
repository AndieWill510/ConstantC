# Thinking Records

ConstantC distinguishes between **essays** and **thinking**.

Essays record constitutional syntheses.

Thinking Records preserve the constitutional deliberation by which those syntheses were earned: questions, hypotheses, objections, revisions, abandoned formulations, and unresolved seams.

The purpose is not to preserve every conversational turn. It is to preserve enough of the intellectual path that a future reader can ask:

- What did ConstantC believe at this stage?
- Why did that formulation fail?
- Which objections changed the work?
- What alternatives were considered?
- What remains unresolved?

Thinking is not disposable preliminary work. It is part of the constitutional record.

Canon without deliberation risks becoming dogma. Deliberation without synthesis never becomes guidance. ConstantC preserves both.

## Form

Each record may contain:

- the question under examination;
- the working hypothesis;
- the strongest challenge raised against it;
- the response or revision;
- remaining constitutional questions.

These files are edited for structure and legibility, but not rewritten to make the path appear straighter than it was.

## Status

Nothing in this directory is canon merely because it appears in the repository. These records document constitutional development. Their authority is evidentiary and deliberative, not final.
